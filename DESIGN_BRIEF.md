Proposed defaults to carry consistently:
- Pending students may log in/read catalog but cannot request until activated. Suspended users cannot log in; librarians can still record returns for them.
- One request is for one title/one copy; staff allocates available copy at approval. No auto approvals or waiting queue for unavailable titles.
- At most 5 pending requests per student, at most 1 pending/approved/active request per title per student; combined reserved+active borrowing cap 5 enforced at approval and pickup. Pending requests do not consume that borrowing allowance.
- Requested and approved durations: whole days 1..30 (proposal, not school policy). Reason required 10..500 characters. Requested duration retained even if librarian changes approved duration; decision note required for rejection or a different approved duration.
- Pickup deadline: approval instant plus 48 hours. Release expired uncollected assignments during ordinary approval/request/listing actions, no cron required. At exact deadline expired. Availability queries exclude expired holds even before cleanup; approval transaction releases conflicting stale assignment before allocation. Loan due date = Manila pickup date + approved days; return allowed through end of due date; overdue only after date has passed.
- Student cancels own pending or approved-uncollected request; librarian may cancel uncollected approvals with reason. No cancel after pickup; no renewals.
- Overdue active loans prevent new requests, approval or pickup, but never prevent return.
- Archive books/copies/accounts instead of deleting history. Do not archive copies with a live hold/loan; archive books only when no live allocations. History retains names/titles via archive (renames affect display, audit records record changes; no immutable metadata snapshots required).
- Cover uploads staff-only JPEG/PNG, max 2 MB, <=4000x4000 pixels, re-encode, random file name, no execution; optional cover has placeholder. No covers needed in seed.
- Display available copies from physical copies and live allocations; not an editable stored count. Adding N copies creates N unique internal IDs/codes; removal archives specific available copies.
- No reports module for MVP: searchable catalog/copy list and current loan/overdue list suffice. Essential audit and backup/restore still required.
- No email, notifications service, reservations/FIFO waitlist, renewals, import/export reporting, fine handling, scanning, e-books or offline sync. Overdue labels and decision status appear on dashboards.
