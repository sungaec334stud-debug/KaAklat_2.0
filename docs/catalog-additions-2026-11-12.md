# Catalog additions and sources — 2026-11-12

User-authorized addition of two real titles in each of the 19 existing categories. Copy quantities (2–5) are randomized simulated pilot holdings, not proof that the university owns physical stock. Reconcile inventory before real lending. These are relevant subject resources, not a claim of Universidad de Dagupan syllabus adoption.

Metadata was checked against publisher, university, author or Project Gutenberg records. Summaries below are short original paraphrases. Publication years follow the existing work-first-publication convention, including the start of serialization; cover editions can differ. Credits for Ernstmeyer/Christman and Morgan-Ellis are editorial roles in their source records.

Covers were obtained from the linked source sites and retained as protected JPEG catalog thumbnails. Four publisher SVG cover cards were rasterized; other images were JPEG/PNG.
