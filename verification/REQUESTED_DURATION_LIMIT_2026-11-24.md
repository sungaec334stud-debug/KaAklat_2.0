# Requested-duration approval limit — 2026-11-24

The previous service accepted any1–30-day approval when a note explained a difference. A test reproduced a7-day request being approved for8 days with a note. The service now rejects approved days greater than the stored requested_days after the existing request lock, identity/pending checks and before any write. Shortening still needs a note, equal duration remains valid, and rejection behavior is unchanged. The existing transaction, roles/session, copy allocation, allowance, overdue and expiry safeguards remain authoritative.

The librarian field defaults to requested_days and sets its per-row maximum accordingly. Linked copy explains the maximum. Client validation blocks an overlong period; a deliberately forged native POST is also rejected by the server and linked to the duration field. The original requested terms remain unchanged. No schema migration, existing-decision update or due-date backfill is performed. Approvals already recorded under the old rule remain historical facts.
